StoreClinic — Privacy Policy

Operated by Brahka Labs. Effective October 31, 2026.

StoreClinic is a conversion audit service for e-commerce stores, available through Muse. It checks whether a store is a fit for an audit, takes audit orders, and reports order status. This policy explains what information the service handles and why.

Information we handle

When you use StoreClinic, you may provide: your name and email address, your store's URL and platform, your main product page URL, and optional details you choose to share about your store (such as approximate monthly visitors, advertising spend, and notes about your concerns). Your store's public pages are also reviewed by a human as part of producing your audit.

We do not ask for, and you should not provide, payment card numbers, passwords, or access credentials to your store through the connector. StoreClinic audits are performed from your store's public pages; we never need your store login.

How we use it

Your information is used only to operate your order: checking audit fit, creating and tracking your order, arranging payment, communicating with you by email about your audit, and producing and delivering your report. A paying customer's store, findings, and report are never used in public content, examples, or published studies without that customer's written consent.

Who else is involved

Hosting: the service runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database), where order records are stored. Payments: payments are processed by Stripe through Stripe payment links. Stripe handles your payment details directly; we never receive or store full card numbers. Email: order communication and report delivery happen by email from hello@storeclinic.co.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties other than the providers above as needed to run the service.

Retention and your choices

Order records are kept so the service can track and support your audit. You may ask for your order data to be deleted at any time by emailing hello@storeclinic.co, and we will delete it unless we are required to retain a record (for example, a payment record) by law.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address. Questions: hello@storeclinic.co.
