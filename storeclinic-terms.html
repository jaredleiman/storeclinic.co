StoreClinic — Terms of Service

The service

StoreClinic is a conversion audit service for live e-commerce stores, available through Muse. It can describe the audit options, check whether a store is a fit for an audit, take an audit order and payment, and report the status of an existing order. Every audit report is produced by a human from your store's public pages and delivered by email.

Fees and delivery

The Conversion Teardown costs $349 and is delivered within 48–72 hours; the Growth Audit costs $799, adds a Loom video walkthrough, and is delivered within 48 hours on a priority basis. Delivery windows start when payment is received. You pay through a Stripe payment link presented to you in advance; you are never charged without seeing the amount first.

Upgrade credit

If you buy the Conversion Teardown ($349) and upgrade to the Growth Audit ($799) within 14 days of your Teardown report being delivered, the full $349 is credited toward the Growth Audit, so you pay the $450 difference. To claim the credit, email hello@storeclinic.co. One credit per Teardown order; a refunded Teardown has no credit to apply.

The guarantee

If your report contains fewer than 3 specific actionable fixes, you receive a full refund of the audit fee. The guarantee applies to both audit tiers.

To claim the guarantee, email hello@storeclinic.co with your order number within 30 days of delivery. We check the report against the test above and decide within 2 business days. Approved refunds are paid through Stripe to the original payment method.

What an audit is — and is not

An audit is a written professional assessment with recommendations. It is not implementation: changes to your store are yours to make. Implementation help is not something StoreClinic sells today: the report's 7-item action plan is ordered by effort, and each item says exactly what to change and why, so you or your developer can implement it directly from the report. Recommendations are based on your store's public pages at the time of review. StoreClinic does not guarantee any particular revenue, conversion-rate, or sales outcome.
