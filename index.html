Questions

What exactly do I get in the audit report?
A full PDF report (8 to 14 pages depending on tier) covering: an executive summary of your biggest conversion blockers, a revenue impact analysis showing money left on the table, scored analysis of your offer and pricing structure, product page copy diagnosis with specific fix suggestions, a trust and credibility gap review, a 7-item prioritized action plan labeled by effort level, and "The One Thing", the single highest-leverage move you can make this week. Premium adds a 15 to 20 minute Loom video walkthrough of your actual store.
How is this different from a generic CRO checklist?
Every audit is specific to your store, your offer, and your products. We visit your store as a real customer would, read your actual product copy, analyze your pricing structure, and give you recommendations that reference your specific headlines, CTAs, and offer mechanics. No templates. No "add social proof" without telling you exactly what kind and where.
What platforms and store types do you audit?
Any live ecommerce store on Shopify, WooCommerce, Squarespace, Wix, or BigCommerce. The conversion principles, clear offer, strong price anchoring, urgency, and risk reversal, apply across niches like beauty, skincare, wellness, apparel, supplements, home goods, and outdoor gear. Your store just needs to be live and generating at least some traffic for the audit to be useful.
What if I want help implementing the fixes?
Not from us. Implementation help is not something StoreClinic sells today. The report is built to stand on its own: the 7-item action plan is ordered by effort, and each item says exactly what to change and why, so you or your developer can implement it directly from the report.
What's the money-back guarantee?
If we deliver your audit and you don't find at least 3 specific, actionable fixes you can implement, not generic advice, but specific recommendations for your actual store, we'll refund 100% of your payment. No questions asked. We're confident enough in the quality that we're happy to put our money behind it.
How do I know this will actually lift my conversions?
We can't guarantee a specific conversion rate increase, that depends on your traffic quality, your product, and how quickly you implement. What we guarantee is a specific, accurate diagnosis of what's preventing conversions right now. A store converting at 1.4%, the Shopify average in Littledata's benchmark data, and lifting to 2.8 to 3%+ doubles revenue from identical traffic. The fixes are real. The results depend on how fast you act on them.
