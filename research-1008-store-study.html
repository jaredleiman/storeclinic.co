We checked 1,008 stores. Here is what we found.

In October and November 2026, StoreClinic read 1,008 live online stores the way a first-time buyer reads them: the homepage, the featured product, the policy pages, the signup forms. No accounts, no back-end access. This page is the full count of what was missing.

The short version: most stores in the crawl were missing basics a shopper looks for before paying. 593 of 1,008 stores (58.8%) had no findable shipping page. 521 (51.7%) showed no product reviews at all. 479 (47.5%) had no email signup we could find. And 270 (26.8%) displayed a sale price that was exactly the regular price. The full findings, and how we counted, are below.

How we counted

The crawl ran in October and November 2026 and covers 1,008 stores where every check on this page could be completed. Stores where a check could not be completed are not in the count.

Each store was read from its public pages only, following the path a first-time buyer follows: the homepage, the featured product, the footer policy links, and any signup form. We used no customer accounts, no logins, and no back-end access.

A page counts as missing when a buyer following the store’s own visible links could not find it. A sale counts as fake when the sale price shown was identical to the regular price shown next to it. The review count is about what a visitor can see on the product page, not about what a store may collect somewhere else.

For outside context: Littledata puts the average Shopify store conversion rate at 1.4%, with the top 10% of Shopify stores at 4.7% (Littledata, via Blend Commerce, updated November 2026). Those two numbers are Littledata’s. Every other number on this page is from our own crawl.
